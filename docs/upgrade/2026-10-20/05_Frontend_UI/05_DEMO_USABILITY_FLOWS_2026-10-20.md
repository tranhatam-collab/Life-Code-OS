# Demo and usability flows — amendment 2026-10-20

All demos use synthetic, visibly labeled fixtures. A demo proves only the recorded build and flow; it is not production or approval evidence. Each flow must be run at 390×844 and 1280×800, keyboard-only, and at least once with the agreed screen reader. Network/error variants are mandatory, not bonus scenes.

## Moderated usability protocol

Use 5–8 participants per materially different audience for formative rounds; sample size is a research plan, not statistical proof. Ask participants to explain scope and provenance, complete the task without coaching, recover from one injected failure, and locate correction/export/delete. Record task completion, critical error, assistance, time only where meaningful, comprehension and qualitative confusion. Do not collect real birth/health/relationship data for a demo.

A flow pauses when a participant can accidentally disclose data, authorize the wrong subject, believe pending payment/report work is complete, cannot recover without data loss, or cannot complete a critical task with keyboard/screen reader. Cosmetic findings may proceed under an owned backlog; safety, authorization, payment, privacy and critical accessibility findings may not.
