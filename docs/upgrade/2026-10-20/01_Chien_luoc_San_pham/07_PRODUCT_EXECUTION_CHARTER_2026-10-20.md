# Product execution charter — amendment 2026-10-20

Document role: executable amendment to the six product-strategy documents dated 2026-10-11. It does not delete or narrow their vision. Evidence status: `NOT_CHECKED`; every launch remains conditional on decisions, gates and receipts. Decision state is inherited from `DECISION_REGISTER`: Q1–Q20 are `PENDING_FOUNDER`.

## 1. Product promise and first operating boundary

Life Code OS helps an adult understand which data and methods were used, distinguish fact from interpretation, choose a reversible action, and revisit the result. It must not present symbolic methods as diagnosis, destiny, measured worth, or investment/employment advice.

The first pilot is adults acting for themselves. Minors, third-party profiles, health inference, biometric/face/hand/foot analysis, employer high-impact decisions, public autonomous publishing and international commerce stay closed until their dedicated legal, method, security and operating gates have current evidence.

Success is not “the report feels right.” A usable outcome requires all of the following:

- the person can identify the input, source/method version, uncertainty and limitations;
- the person can correct or withdraw data and revoke future sharing;
- one action can be chosen, owned, time-bounded and later reviewed;
- the same permitted input and release produces a reproducible typed result;
- private content is not disclosed through cache, analytics, URL, logs or authorization failures.

## 3. Proposed outcome and kill metrics

Targets below are hypotheses for pilot design, not current measurements or approvals. Q20 must approve cohorts, thresholds, observation window and budget before a metric can open or kill a release.

| Metric ID | Definition | Proposed pilot target | Proposed pause/kill trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| PM-01 | Scope comprehension: participant correctly identifies what the output does and does not say | at least 85% in moderated task | below 70% after one copy revision |
| PM-02 | Data/method lineage retrieval | 100% sampled claims expose input class, method release and source/limitation | any published sampled claim lacks lineage |
| PM-03 | Action usefulness | at least 70% can select one specific reversible action without moderator rescue | below 50% or repeated coercive/fear feedback |
| PM-04 | Correction completion | at least 90% of sampled users can correct a fact and see a new version | any correction mutates prior published snapshot or loses audit history |
| PM-05 | Privacy boundary | zero cross-subject, cross-tenant or revoked-grant disclosures in negative suite | any reproducible unauthorized disclosure |
| PM-06 | Accessibility | no critical WCAG 2.2 AA failure in critical flows; keyboard and screen-reader completion recorded | any blocker prevents authentication, consent, payment, report or privacy-right completion |
| PM-07 | Report integrity | deterministic numeric/date assertions match fixtures; unsupported assertion rate 0 in reviewed sample | any high-risk unsupported health, destiny, employment or financial claim |
| PM-08 | Reliability | threshold and window set by SLO decision; job has bounded retry/cancel/recovery | unbounded retry, duplicate charge/report, or unreconciled terminal job |
| PM-09 | Service sustainability | contribution, reviewer capacity, support time and AI cost measured per eligible completed outcome | budget cap exceeded without graceful deterministic fallback, or backlog breaches approved service window |
| PM-10 | Voluntary retention | measured without dark patterns; cohort and window approved before use | retention relies on fear, data lock-in or blocked privacy rights |

No aggregate “readiness percentage” may be computed from these metrics. A single safety or authorization kill trigger keeps the affected release at `HOLD` regardless of positive conversion.

## 4. Stage contract

1. **Discover/registry (P0–A):** source, rights, claims, purposes, excluded users and decision dependencies recorded. Exit requires G01/G04/G06 evidence, not merely a document.
2. **Trust foundation (B):** identity, object/tenant/subject authorization, consent, grants, typed data and reversible jobs. Exit requires negative authorization and privacy-right tests.
3. **Build and synthetic demo (C):** responsive PWA and API contracts operate only on synthetic fixtures. Exit requires traceable tests for every in-scope screen and operation.
4. **Private reviewed pilot (D):** invited adult/self-first cohort, bounded payment and human report review. Exit requires reconciliation, support, correction, incident and rollback receipts.
5. **Measured expansion (E):** products open one contract at a time after differentiated evidence and capacity checks. Design completion never auto-opens collection or sale.
6. **Advanced/global (F):** high-sensitivity levels, organizations, API and countries only after their dedicated legal/security/operating decisions. The long-term vision stays registered even while launch remains closed.

Conditional labels in the 2026-10-11 catalog resolve as follows: `F_REVIEW` = F plus specialist/method review; `E_F_REVIEW` = design in E and launch only after F review; `F_LEGAL` = F plus country/legal approval. They are not additional universal phases.

## 5. Ownership and decision dependencies

Roles are accountable functions, not invented people. Founder decides product philosophy and Q1–Q20 within applicable functional review. Product Lead owns outcomes and scope; Tech Lead owns integration; Privacy/Legal owns lawful-purpose and country constraints; Method Council owns releaseable methods; Data/Backend owns typed results and isolation; Frontend/UX owns screen behavior and accessibility; AI/Editorial owns sourced synthesis; QA/Security independently verifies; SRE owns release/restore evidence; Finance/Commerce owns approved pricing and cost model.

No role name here constitutes approval. Every approval must record baseline, scope, conditions, approver identity and time in the decision/evidence system.

## 6. Estimation and cost discipline

The original 140 tasks estimate 361–852 person-days. The 2026-10-20 amendment adds explicit contract/trace/demo work, recorded separately as `UPG-*`; it does not rewrite the original estimate. Program cost is calculated only as:

`labor = Σ(person-days by role × approved loaded day rate by role)`

`total planning envelope = labor + approved vendor/legal/content/accessibility/security costs + contingency`

Currency, vendor prices and loaded rates remain `null` until Q14/Q15/Q20 decisions. Finance must publish low/base/high rates, tax treatment and contingency before any money estimate is called approved. Person-day estimates are planning ranges, not delivery promises; legal review, Founder decisions and gate evidence sit on the critical path and cannot be compressed by adding engineers.

## 7. Required decision packet per product

Each product-opening decision must include: product/release IDs; eligible subject and purpose; allowed and excluded claims; required inputs and null behavior; methods and source/rights releases; screens/API operations; desktop/mobile/PWA parity evidence; data/retention/cache policy; report/reviewer contract; price/quota/cost assumptions if commercial; support/SLO/rollback; success and kill metrics; country/locale; gate receipts; and explicit approvers. Missing evidence is `BLOCKED` or `NOT_CHECKED`, never zero and never implied approval.
